# Victoria's Institutions



## The High Institute

The High Institute is home to the seats of power in Victoria. The city is governed by a group of elites calling themselves "The Syndicate". In the High Institute you can find the residences of the Syndicate members and their families. Any matter relating to law and politics, is to be discussed in "The High Institute". 
Since the law is issued has its practice controlled by the syndicate, every Victorian lawyer works at "The High Institute".
Citizens can get a lawyer from "The High Institute", and the defense and judge will also be issued by that same institute.
Civil administration, anything involving permits, public works such as construction or plumbing, sanitation etc. All of these are also issued and managed by "The High Institute".
The High Institute members are also the ones who control the banks. To become a syndicate member you must have a solid background and be one of the major shareholders of the city. The city's major shareholders control the banks and this gives them the ultimate governing power.


## Banks

Victoria is home to 3 banks. Every one offers different financial products. There is one located in the 2nd layer of the city, one in the 4th layer and one in the 6th layer. Banks in Victoria are essentially a subsidiary of "The High Institute". The people sitting on the board of the banks are members of the families forming the syndicate. They offer predatory loans to the poorest and the best rates to their elite friends. 
The banks control the debt collectors who have a duty to arrest the citizens who reach the bankruptcy state.


## The Assembly Locals
These offices, can be found in the 5th layer of the city. The ones occupying the rooms are the major players of the city. This includes; Major shareholders of industry, custodians of cultural entities, land owners who contribute to the city's resources, business owners with a certain pedigree.
Anybody's who is anyone has a office in the Assembly Locals or a proxy if they are of too high standing. The greater families will often have at least one member working at this location.


## The Longevity Center

"The Longevity Center", is the name of the hospital in Victoria. There is only one hospital but it is spacious and has plenty room. The longevity center, is where Victorians go for health treatments, operations examination, everything to do with medical doctors. The Longevity Center also is the provider of elixirs and other medical concoctions, available at one of the three clinics in the City.
The Longevity Center is not a free service. Citizens often go in debt to pay for its services.

## Academy

The academy, creates the future of Victoria. The people frequenting the academy are often children of powerful people or very bright individuals who are being groomed for leadership. 
The academy doesn't simply educate it also contributes to advancements made in the city. Scientific and technological advancement are made in the research wing of the academy. 
Many of the magical tools and industrial techniques used in Victoria where developed in the academy. 

## Law Enforcement Barracks

The law enforcement barracks are the home of the policing agents of Victoria. The police officers meant to keep order reside there as well as the debt collectors who are officials in the eyes of Victoria. 
Fortunately for Victorians they are different departments and will very rarely intervene in each others business.

## Newspaper

The newsroom is a lively place where 3 journalist companies work to bring their version of the truth to the citizens. One paper focuses on finances, the other on social gossip and the last one on the hardships the city is facing.
