

# Roles

### Suppliers

The suppliers in Victoria are companies, corporations and co who belong to the "Primary Sector". 

### Manufacturers

The manufacturers are companies and corporations who exist within many "Sectors of Industry", notably; [Manufacturing Sector, Transformation Sector, Energy Sector]. A minor one still worth mentioning is "Market Goods Sector".

### Distributors

The relevent players in this category are the Market Goods Secotr entities.
