

# Roles

### Suppliers

The suppliers are the industries who create something another industry relies upon to function properly. Suppliers are at the basis of the marketplace. Without them nothing is moving. The major "Sectors of Industry" with supplier businesses are; [Primary Sector, Market Goods Sector, Manufacturing Sector, Transformation Sector, Logistics Sector, Energy Secot].

### Manufacturers

The manufacturers are companies and corporations who exist within many "Sectors of Industry", notably; [Manufacturing Sector, Transformation Sector, Logistics Sector, Energy Sector]. A minor one still worth mentioning is "Market Goods Sector".



### Distributors

Distributors are a the ones through which goods are acquired. All distributors have a physical location you can interact with in the game and are the physical representation of the marketplace in the "Debt & Desire" game. Industries who are not distributors do not have a physical location you can interact with in game.
The relevant players in this category are; [Market Goods Sector, Manufacturing Sector, Transformation Sector, Logistics Sector, Energy Sector].

## Services

Services are also physically present in game only you don't interact with the location of the service but rather the NPC's responsible for the services. The players in "Debt & Desire", make mana stones which is a form of energy. Being a player in the energy sector, the player needs to interact with the NPC's who sell services to potentially sell them mana stones as energy to aid in their service. All actors who belong to an industry with the service function automatically count as customers for the players. Industries with other functions can be customers but for 'service' it's always true.
