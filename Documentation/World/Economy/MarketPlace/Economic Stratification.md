


# TIERS


## Low End

Low end products are the worst type of product a Victorian can buy and no service exist in the low range price ranges. Entrepreneurs in Victoria don't set out to service the poorest. The low range price range is largely made available through House Corporation owned businesses.

## Mid Range

Mid range products & services are the most accessible kind of purchase an average Victorian can make if he or she wants to look presentable, be healthy, have a good life. Mid range products can even be bought by a rich person though they rather not.


## High End

High end product & services represent the upper class consumer. Its where the noble and rich merchants will go to buy goods not accessible to the lower class. You can find much of the same products you will find in the markets only with the addition of items such as jewelry, perfume, watches and other luxury goods. The "Sectors of Industry" the "Luxury Stores" depend on are;

- Primary Sector
- Market Goods Sector
- Transformation Sector
- Logistics Sector
