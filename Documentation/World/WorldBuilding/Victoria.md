
Victoria, is the name of the city where "Debt & Desire" takes place. 
The city lies within a large basin. A wide cavity formed within a mountain with a grand opening to enter at the front while a crescent shaped cliff barrier forms a natural barrier along the back of the city. Waterfalls flow down the cliff and feed the basin. This water source helped shape the form of the basin through years of erosion. 
At the base of the basin, a network of canals redirect the water excess outside of the city area and also strategically distributes the water through the city by using the sewers, plumbing and gutters. These canals help the city take advantage of its natural source of water in the most optimal manner making water part of Victoria's infrastructure.



# City Info

## Architecture

The architecture in Victoria takes most of its inspiration from the 19th century Victorian Styles. The Victorian influences are meant to give a feeling of familiarity to the player. The city has an air of old Europe yet is a fictitious metropolis with magic at its center.

The city is divided in layers. Every layer, can be identified by a tall and wide arcade retaining terraces lining the city. Every terrace retaining wall rises from the layer below giving the city its characteristic stacked appearance.

Each retaining terrace forms a monumental architectural boundary between layers of the city. Large arcades are integrated into these walls, creating openings through which staircases and other circulation routes connect one level to the next.

### Urban Layout

The city contains seven layers defined by the arcade retaining terraces. The vertical organization of the city mirrors its social hierarchy: the lower levels are denser, more compressed and irregular, while the upper levels become increasingly spacious, orderly and monumental. This creates an upper vs lower city dynamic that can be identified at first glance. The layout of the buildings was set up taking inspiration from "Old Quebec". Particularly its dense urban fabric. Buildings stand close to one another often forming continuous walls along streets. This leaves very little space between neighboring structures. This Canadian city retains this architectural style from the colonial era and the layout of the town has this proximity quality.

Lower levels achieve density through proximity and compression. Upper levels achieve density through larger, more efficiently organized buildings.

### Visual Style

The visual style of the architecture is inspired by the "Italianate" Victorian style. Roof edges projecting out from the walls, the tall and narrow windows sometimes with sometimes without arches at the top. These qualities largely influence the style of the square/rectangular based houses. 

Italianate is not the sole visual style influencing the design of the city. There is also Romanesque revival for its cylindrical shapes, second empire buildings and the very recognizable Haussmann architectural style. 

Romanesque revival, contributes visual references for cylindrical shapes and arches.
Second Empire and Haussmann architecture, are reserved for the upper class buildings. Haussmann architecture plays a significant role in the visual identity of these buildings. Its ordered façades and efficient use of space allow large numbers of accommodations to exist within relatively compact buildings. This makes it particularly suited to Victoria's upper levels, where buildings are given more space while still maintaining a high degree of density. Haussmann is a perfect candidate to preserve the compact look while allowing more space the the richer Victorians.

The Larger building in the city representing the seats of power, has the privilege of having more Gothic influences than the others. This allows the larger building to be more imposing and intimidating.
