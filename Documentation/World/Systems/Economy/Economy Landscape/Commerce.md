[[Sectors of Industry]]

The commerce sector in "Debt & Desire", is a special Sector. 
Unlike the other sectors in the "Sectors of Industry", this one does not produce or transform any commodity nor does it contribute to the city's infrastructure. Rather its a Sector where the distribution and trade of the output of all other sectors happen. 
Hence all other sectors depend on commerce for financial health and positive cash flow and vice versa. The healthy sector see's its output distributed and traded in the commerce sector. The exception being the development sector which depends on the institution known as "Government".

## Markets

Markets represent general consumer retail. Anything from foods to clothing and furniture, can be found in the markets. The "Sectors of Industry" the markets depend on are;

- Primary Sector
- Market Goods Sector
- Manufacturing Sector
- Transformation Sector


## Workshops

Workshops, represent specialized retail, repair and skilled trades. The Workshop focuses on one material. Wood, stone, metal etc. If you want to acquire furniture, decorations for buildings or materials for production, you can visit the workshops and find what you need there. ## Stores 

Stores are specialized retail like workshops only they don't specialize in a material they focus on magic tools, chemicals, etc.


## Offices

Offices are where contract negotiation takes place. The owner of a business can often be found in an office.

## Trading Houses

The trading houses are where the people go to buy company stock. You can go there to buy, sell and get stock appraised. The "Sectors of Industry" the "Trading Houses" depend on are;

- Primary Sector
- Market Goods Sector
- Manufacturing Sector
- Transformation Sector
- Development Sector
- Logistics Sector
- Energy Sector


## Factories

Factories deal in mass production and are off world structures. Many factories exist in the world as assets for organizations to own. Their impact is felt on the market.

## Institutions

Some institutions in Victoria have commercial purposes. For example the academy who acts as a research facility as well as an educational space, has a store selling chemicals because the academy produces some. 

## Public Venues

Many public venues are commercial businesses. Such as restaurants, bars, hostels. Some public venues also contain store like the library.
