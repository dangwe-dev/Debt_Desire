


## House Corporations

A "House Corporation", is an entity containing the assets, businesses and investments of a great family. House Corporations are the ultimate form of corporate success one can achieve in Victoria. To become a House Corporation, you must be a part of a house and own a certain amount of land. The land you own must greatly contribute to the city's economy and you must receive a permit from the syndicate to establish your corporation.
House Corporations, are not restricted by debt when it comes to establishing and maintaining themselves. Their syndicate friends tend to bail them out. To affect their ability to do business, their relationships with the syndicate must be negatively influenced.


## Commercial Corporations

A "Commercial Corporation", is an entity that contains assts, businesses and investments. The "Commercial Corporation", is managed by a board of members. Anyone can become a board member provided they become a major shareholder of the company. 
To create a commercial corporation, the company must receive a permit issued by the syndicate and <span style="color:rgb(200, 30, 150)">have a certain track record when it comes to Debt.</span> (The inner workings of the debt system has yet to be defined but I know it is going to have a major impact on if a commercial Corp can be established).


## CO-Operatives

A co-operative, is a entity created from the alliance of suppliers and manufacturers. Companies who extract resources from the earth or raise cattle as well as those who transform those resources without distributing them. These are the actors creating co-operatives. Co-Operatives in Victoria formed out of need. These co-operatives all have the same clients. House Corporations. House Corporations are the ones who support co-operatives because all of the land in Victoria is owned by a House Corporation or belongs the the state which is essentially "The High Institute. Since co-operatives are a collection of suppliers and manufacturers who deal in with natural resources, its in the interest of the House Corporation to keep them happy and profitable because it profits themselves. There can be many co-operatives one a single land their success is tied to the relationship they have with the Great Families who own the land they operate on. Great Families can let go of an organization due to bad relationship and fund a different entity. So co-operatives formed out of the need of small scale financial players to negotiate with these important House Corporations.


## Chartered Firms

"Chartered Firms" are very similar to commercial corporations. They can own assets, businesses and make investments. They are managed by a board of members and sitting on the board can be achieved through shareholding. A permit, is also necessary for the establishment of a "Chartered Firm". The nuance is a chartered firm is limited to one industry. The investments it makes can't be too varied. If they deal in the transport industry, all of its dealings are restricted to that industry. The advantage of a chartered firm from an entrepreneurial point of view, is that credit checks are much softer and lenient.


## Partnerships

Partnerships are the simplest form of corporate entity. A partnership, does not need a permit to exist. It only needs the necessary permits to operate in its given industry.
Partnerships tend to operate at the small business level. Its rare for a partnership to have tradable actions in the stock market. More often than not partnerships are long term investments that can develop into a commercial corporation or a chartered firm.
