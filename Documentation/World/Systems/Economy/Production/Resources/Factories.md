[[Estates]]


Factories in Victoria are located on industrial lands reserved for the city. They are not on private property unlike the estates. Like the estates, they don't have a physical location in game. They are off world structures that deal in mass production. Their purpose are to make the economy cohesive and coherent.
Many factories exist in the world as assets for organizations to own. Their impact is felt on the market.


## Role

Factories are the main source of supply for the commercial entities in the game. Every market, store, workshop etc. Will get their main source of supply from factories.
