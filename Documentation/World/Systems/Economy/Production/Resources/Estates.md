For the "Debt & Desire" world to be strong and cohesive, it is necessary to have a strong foundation where the economy of the world is concerned. The whole concept, is for the player to be able to influence the world economy with his actions. 
For this to be realized, the economy must have very clear settings. This will allow the development process to easily create the dynamics allowing the player to predict the results of his actions over the economy in the game.

The estates in Victoria, serve as the base of the entire resource based economy. Those who control the estates, control the economy and are the seat of power, (The Syndicate. Every estates is located on one out of six types of lands. These types of lands all produce different kinds of resources that aliment the industries of the city.


# Biomes & Resources


The resources in the city just like in any other, are at the core of everything. The resources are used in many industries so the entities who have control over them are essentially the most powerful groups in the our world.
The lands where these resources hail from are separated into six big categories and these categories when brought together make up the entire economical landscape of the "Debt & Desire" world.
These three categories are:

- Fields
- Pastures
- Forests
- Highlands
- Waters
- Wetlands


### Fields

Fields are lands with wide meadows and extensive agricultural acreage. Fields are lands mainly used for agricultural purposes.

### Pastures

Lands with vast amounts of pastures are mostly used to breed animals and for farming.

### Forests

Forests are a great source of foods, fruits and a variety of plants but mainly they provide wood.

### Highlands

Highlands are mountain like regions where mining and excavating takes place.

## Waters

These lands, are where the fishing and diving takes place.

## Wetlands

Wet lands are home to minerals and other resources you can find in humid lands.



# Biome Distribution


Biomes refer to the different types of land found on estates. every estate, has 5 active biome slots. Every slot can be occupied by one of the 6 biomes available in the system.
The distribution process is done by a loop that starts out selecting a random biome and then looping back to select another one until the 5 slots are filled. Each slot selection decreases the likeliness of the next slot getting a different biome. 
Different oligarchic titles provide average outcomes for lands associated with themselves. 
Meaning every title carries an average of biome variety. The highest titles are more likely to have 5 different biomes active on their lands and the lowest titles are lucky to get 2. No land can contain all of the 6 biomes. 

As Estates are generated the first biome selected for the estate depends on the variety of biomes active on other estates.
The system as a whole also respects a value representing the minimum required flavor of biomes active at the beginning of the game. 
This way all biomes are present at the start of the game with higher titles being more likely to have richer lands and lower titles modest lands.
