For the "Debt & Desire" world to be strong and cohesive, it is necessary to have a strong foundation where the economy of the world is concerned. The whole concept, is for the player to be able to influence the world economy with his actions. 
For this to be realized, the economy must have very clear settings. This will allow the development process to easily create the dynamics allowing the player to predict the results of his actions over the economy in the game.



# Economy Base




## Resources

The resources in the city just like in any other, are at the core of everything. The resources are used in many industries so the entities who have control over them are essentially the most powerful groups in the our world.
The lands where these resources hail from are separated into three big categories and these categories when brought together make up the entire economical landscape of the "Debt & Desire" world.
These three categories are:

- Earth Products
- Agricultural Products
- Animal Products


### Earth Resources

The resources in this categories all hail from the ground. 
Every natural resource that belongs to the earth is in this product category.

- Wood
- Stone
- Metals
- Minerals & Ores
- Coal
- Oil
- Sand & Gravel
- Clay


### Agricultural Resources

The resources in this category also hail from the ground only they are consumables and have to do with nutrition. 

- Food Crops
	- Fruits
	- Vegetables
	- Spices
	- Grain
	- Legumes
	- Roots & Tubers
	- Nuts & Seeds
	- Sugar Crops
	- Oil Crops

- Industrial Crops
	- Cotton
	- Flax
	- Hemp
	- Jute


### Animal Resources

The resources in this category come from livestock.

- Wool
- Silk
- Leather
- Fur
- Meat
- Eggs
- Milk
- Fats
- Bone 
- Honey
- Manure
- Beeswax


### Water Resources

The resources in this category have to do with water.

- Aquatic Foods
	- Fish
	- Shellfish
	- Aquatic Plants
	- Fish oil
	- Algae
- Fresh Water
- Mana Rich Water
