 [[Sectors of Industry]]

The industry sectors in "Debt & Desire" is another foundational layer of the economy. The Industry is heavily dependent on the resources of the world. Whatever happens to the resources will have a butterfly effect like impact on the city's industries. These industries, are separated into 24 different entities. Together, these 24 entities make up the industrial landscape of the "Debt & Desire" City.


# Major Industries

The major sectors of industry contain all of the necessary sectors for the city to function properly.
