# Cultural Entities



## Museum

The museum of Victoria is located in at the 4rth layer of the city. Its architectural style, is Victorian Italianate mixed with the Haussmann architectural style.
The Museum is managed by board of aristocratic members. 
Access to the museum is limited. There is a daily fee you need to pay to enter and visit. This fee gives you daily access but no more. You will have to pay again the next day. 
This fee allows the richest and the comfortable members of society to access the museum without worrying about encountering poorer individuals. 

The museum, displays the city's history and its magical creations. There is a floor for history, a floor for arts and sculptures and finally a floor for magical tools and artifacts.

The museum, is a non-profit organization. The rich families in the city have a duty to donate to the cultural institutions. This duty maintains the prestige of the city and creates an opportunity for philanthropy.


## Libraries

There are 3 libraries in Victoria. One is located on the 4rth layer of the city, the other is on the 6th layer of the city and the last one is located in the 7th layer. 
The first location is meant for all, the second is also accessible only the fee is higher and it is really meant for the richest people hence its location in the higher layers. The last, is only accessible to the ruling class but its possible to get a special pass to access it. Otherwise you will have to get in by infraction if there is crucial information you want.
Libraries, like the museum of Victoria are are also a non-profit organization but still require a fee for access.


## Theatre

The theatre is a cultural attraction in the city of Victoria. The high minded posh people in Victoria appreciate the arts and love knowledge. Victoria being a magical and knowledgeable city, its population appreciates a good story and this service can be found in the theatres. The theatre uses many magical tools to help with the immersion and spectacle. The theatre is reserved for the wealthy though every now and then there will be a festival during which the theatre will give outside performances accessible to all.
The theatre, is located in the 5th layer of the city and every visit requires a hefty payment.

## Bars / Cocktail Bars

Bars are a good place to make friends. The bars are where people go to relax and enjoy drinks. Up to the 4rth layer you can find regular bars but once you reach the higher levels, you will only find cocktail bars meant to attract richer people. Cocktail bars are pricier and are visited by citizens with more means.

## Hostels

There are two hostels in Victoria. One in the 3rd layer and one in the 6th layer. 
Victoria wants its more elevated tourists to appreciate more luxury so even middle class people will be discriminated against. Putting the hostel in the 6th layer ensures only very financially comfortable people will access it the rest can go stay at the 3rd layer even if they have the means to pay for the higher things in Victoria they still won't sleep next to the elite.

## Parks

There are 4 parks in Victoria. One park is located in the 2nd layer, one in the 5th layer, one in the 6th and the last one is in the 7th layer.

The parks are not accessible to all. The one in the 2nd can be accessed by all though really it's meant to be used by the poorest. The one in the 5th layer can only be access by those who bought the pass. A certain amount is deducted from your accounts every day to pay for access to the park in the 5th layer. The one in the 6th layer also as a pass you need to pay for daily only you must also have a high enough title to frequent that area. The one in the 7th layer is accessed by those who pay the fee, have a required title, and also are major shareholders in the city's top industries/companies.
