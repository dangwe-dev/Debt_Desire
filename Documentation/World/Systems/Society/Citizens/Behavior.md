[[Citizen Profiles]]


Moods are the secret behind the NPC AI system governing the Victorians. The entire demography of Victoria is governed by moods. These moods affect how the Victorians react to the player and between themselves. 



# SELF ESTEEM


Self esteem is a core component of the behavior of NC's in Victoria. The esteem the characters have for themselves can go from very positive to very negative. This affects their entire approach to social life and investments. A Victorian with poor self esteem is less likely to engage in conversation and invest in ventures. Whereas a citizen with high confidence can be convinced to do almost anything provided you cultivate the right affinity with them.

Esteem can be read through a 'UI' component visible when interacting with a citizen exposing "Confidence" for very positive self esteem and "Defeated" for very poor self esteem.


# AFFINITY

Affinities, describe the state of relationship between the player and an NPC it also describes how they view you depending on the context.
Three values make up the affinity system for the citizens including the player.
[Credibility, Respect, Appreciation].

Every affinity dynamics has its own rewards and punishments.


## Credibility

Credibility tells the player whether or not they have the trust of the person they are interacting with. 

TRUST < - > SUSPICION


## Respect

Every relationship does not maintain the same level of respect. Some people might respect you more than others. Situation the player finds himself in can greatly impact the respect of the witnesses. Most interaction affect the level of respect the person you interacted with feels towards you.

ADMIRATION < - > DISGUST


## Appreciation

Not every citizen likes the player. Appreciation indicates whether someone wants the best for you or if they wish you harm

GRATITUDE < - > ENVY
