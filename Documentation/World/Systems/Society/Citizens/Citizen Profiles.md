

# Identity

Every citizen in Victoria has a permanent identity provided they don't die. Identity, is defined by name, family, age etc. 

## NAMES

Names, are generated from a controlled random pool of possible names. Every social class has a pool of possible names to give out to Victorian Citizens. Social class determines what names are accessible to citizens.

## Family

Family names, just like names, are randomly given out to every family members the system generates. The names are picked from a pool of available words. This strategy ensures the same families and citizens are not permanent across many different games. Only the rules remain. Social class determines which available names can be given out to families.

## Age

Age does have some weight in Debt & Desire. It influences the probability of income and the types of relationships the citizens have. Its one of the mechanics adding flavor and depth to the world.
