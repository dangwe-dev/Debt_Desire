

# Identity

Every citizen in Victoria has a permanent identity provided they don't die. Identity, is defined by name, family, age etc. 

## NAMES

Names, are generated from a controlled random pool of possible names. Every social class has a pool of possible names to give out to Victorian Citizens. 

## Family

Family names, just like names, are randomly given out to every family members generated. The names are picked from a pool of available words. This strategy ensures the same families and citizens are not permanent across many different games. Only the rules remain.

## Age

Age does have some weight in Debt & Desire. It influences the probability of income and the types of relationships the citizens have
