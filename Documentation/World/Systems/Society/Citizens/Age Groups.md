
Victoria, counts 4 age groups in town. Age group are an important organizational metric in the game. They help diversify the visual look of families and also serve as a filter for title assignment and family head selection.

The four age groups are as such:

- #### Elders
- #### Adults
- #### Teenagers
- #### Children

Aside from children, the other groups have income.
Children are limited in the amount of titles they can hold.

# Age Group Assignment

## General Assignment Rules
### General Age Group Rules

- A family of 1 must have 1 adult.
- A family of 2 must have 1 adult unless its an upper class family.
- Every family of 3 or more must have one male and one female.
  Commoners must have 2 adults at this point. 
- Every family of 4 or more must have a non-adult.
- Every family above 5 must have 2 non-adults. It can be 2 from 2 different groups or from the same.
- If a family has more than 6 members there must be a member from every age group present.

#### These rules are compounded. So every family above 3 will get two adults if the family member count stops at 4 than additionally you will get at least one member from one of the other age groups and so on and so forth.


## Aristocrats & Gentiles
### Upper Class Age Group Rules

- Every aristocratic and gentile family of 2 or more must have at least 1 elder per family at the start of every game. This rule changes the family dynamics of the upper classes and are added to the general age group rules.
  
- The family of "2" rule in the "General Age Group Rules" is replaced by the "Upper Class Age Group Rule" for "2". 
  Meaning for upper class people a family of 2 can be 
  
  [Elder + Elder],
  [Elder + Adult],  
  [Elder + Teenager],  
  [Elder + Child].
  
- Every aristocratic and gentile family of 3 or more must have at least one adult.
  
  [Elder + Random + Adult]
  
- Every aristocratic and gentile family of 4 or more must have at least 1 male and 1 female.
  
  
## Commoners & Vagrants

Commoners and vagrants do not have absolute age group requirements outside of the "General Age Group Rules".
