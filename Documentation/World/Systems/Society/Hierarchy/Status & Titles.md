		  	
The Statuses in Debt & Desire are a metric that gives insight about social class to the players. Every citizen in Victoria has a status describing their social position.
Titles on the other hand, grant different powers and defines behavior.
Middle class titles are infinite but there is a finite number of upper class, and underclass titles. This mechanic ensures the game balances itself. I
f the economy is the heart of the game, titles are its blood.

In addition to being a element allowing the player to predict behavior, titles are responsible for how many businesses exist. The economy of Victoria is driven by the production of its estates. Estates, can only be held by oligarchic title holders. This means the finite number of titles for Oligarchs allows the system to create a finite number of estates to be managed by these upper-class men. Then the lands belonging to the state are occupied by the factories who are mostly owned by gentry folk. The amount of estates influences the amount of factories. (Factories are supplied by estates so there is a limit to how many customers an estate can handle due to its production output).

## <span style="color:rgb(80, 180, 200)">Financial Imperators</span>

The Financial Imperator title is given to to one of the 6 Syndicate members.
This title gives the holder the most coveted power in all of Victoria and that is the power to control the lands, the banks, the titles and permits. Financial Imperators are essentially the ultimate rulers of the city. They also bypass the rule of one noble title per family. Making the families they hail from more powerful than a regular Oligarchic family.

# <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Gentile Titles</span>


## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Banker</span>

Bankers are titles given to Victorian working in the banks. Bankers can give out loans to the players and help them settle all financial needs.
Bankers can belong to the middle class or be gentry folk. The difference between the two types of bankers is the quality and reach of the service they give.

## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Executives</span>

Executives are Victorians who have access to the assembly locals. They are people with high positions of power within their own industries and businesses. Sometimes business owners sometimes shareholders.

## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Business Owner</span>

Business owners differ from executive in a simple way, they do not have access to the exclusive "Assembly Locals". They occupy simple offices.

## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Doctors</span>

Doctors are employed by the Longevity Center and the clinics. Their trade is bringing people back to health.

## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Savants</span>

Savants refer to the researchers and professors of the academy. These personnel members are the innovators, inventors and scientist of Victoria.

## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Debt Collectors</span>

The debt collectors are the main opponents to the players. They start targeting the player when the player is in debt. The higher the debt amount the faster the debt collectors identify the players.


## <span style="color:rgb(150, 255, 150)">Journalist</span>

Journalist gather information about everything happening in Victoria and publish their articles. The newspaper is delivered at the beginning of every day. 



# <span style="color:rgb(255, 230, 100)">Commoner Titles</span>



## <span style="color:rgb(255, 230, 100)">Soldier</span>

Soldiers function in the exact same way debt collectors do only they target criminals instead of citizens.

## <span style="color:rgb(255, 230, 100)">Civil Servant</span>

The Civil Servant title refers to all characters working in public venues such as, the museum and the libraries.


## <span style="color:rgb(255, 230, 100)">Workers</span>

Workers, are employees of businesses found all over Victoria. Commercial and public venues.
Victorians working in markets, workshops, stores, restaurants, the theatre, bars, hostels, the parks etc.



# <span style="color:rgb(200, 90, 200)">Pariah Titles</span>



## <span style="color:rgb(200, 90, 200)">Criminals</span>

Criminals is a title most common among the underclass. The player, can also fall to the level of a criminal. Unfortunately to go from rags to riches in Victoria, you must partake in criminal activity. Stealing the journal to learn about actuality, taking illegal loans to get a leg up in society, all of the different ways of gaining you money back when you are down will involve criminal activity. 
Criminals get active and violent at night. A player caught outside at night will have a hard time surviving.


## <span style="color:rgb(200, 90, 200)">Beggars</span>



Beggars, are members of the underclass not partaking in criminal activity. This makes it impossible for them to rise above their social class. Careful though they do not partake in crime they report stalk and give away your position if you don't regularly donate money to them.
