
The titles in Debt & Desire are a metric that gives insight about social class to the players. Every citizen in Victoria has a title but not every title holds power. 
Middle class titles are infinite but there is a finite number of upper class and underclass titles. This mechanic ensures the game balances itself and if the economy is the heart of the game, titles are its blood.

In addition to being a element allowing the player to decipher social class, titles are responsible for how many businesses exist. The economy of Victoria is driven by the production of its estates. Estates, can only be held by title holders. This means the finite number of titles for Oligarchs allows the system to create a finite number of estates to be managed by the oligarchs. Then the lands belonging to the state are occupied by the factories who are mostly owned by gentry folk. The amount of estates influences the amount of factories. (Factories are supplied by estates so there is a limit to how many customers an estate can handle due to its production output).
