[[Citizen Profiles]]


The population composition of Victoria is maintained by a series of constant foundational metrics. These metrics describe marital status, family size, the weight social classes carry and social composition probabilities.
Every social metric has a minimum and maximum value representing the potential amount of citizens represented within that metric. Every social setting has an average of citizens it needs to hold.

## Locals & Foreigners

The city is in major part composed of local citizens but there are also a portion of foreigners. Foreigners occupy a portion of society ranging from a little under 20% to a little under 10% per class. Each class has its own weight for local and foreign citizens.
