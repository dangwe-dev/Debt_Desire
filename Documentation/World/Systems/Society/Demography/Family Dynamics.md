
Every generated family in Victoria follows a certain set of rules. These rules allow the system to assign titles comfortably and to make family demography consistently varied. 

## HEADS

Firstly there must be a head of the family within every family. The head of the family is the one who makes the decisions affecting the entire family. This means the head is in charge of the wallet. Every family member receives income from their job or a split of the income from the family assets but the one controlling the assets and by defect the investments is the head of the family.


# ORDER

#### The "AGE GROUP " weights first decide which age group will be responsible for providing the head of the family. If there is more than one sex present within that age group, then the "SEX" weights apply.

##### When the both rules have been applied, there might still be two members of the same sex within an age group. This case requires the system to pick the citizen with the lowest "citizen_ID" meaning the citizen generated first.


### Aristocrats & Gentiles

#### Head Assignment
For the upper classes of Victoria, the probabilities favor elders and males.
 Children cannot be head of families.


| AGE GROUPS                  | ELDERS | ADULTS | TEENS | CHILDREN |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ----- | -------- |
| CHANCE of BEING<br>the HEAD | 70%    | 20%    | 10%   | 0%       |

| SEX                         | MALES | FEMALES |
| --------------------------- | ----- | ------- |
| CHANCE of BEING<br>the HEAD | 70%   | 30%     |

### Commoners & Vagrants

#### Head Assignment
For the middle and lower classes of Victoria, the probabilities favor the adults.
Males and females have an equal chance of being heads of families.


| AGE GROUPS                  | ELDERS | ADULTS | TEENS | CHILDREN |
| --------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ----- | -------- |
| CHANCE of BEING<br>the HEAD | 30%    | 50%    | 20%   | 0%       |

| SEX                         | MALES | FEMALES |
| --------------------------- | ----- | ------- |
| CHANCE of BEING<br>the HEAD | 50%   | 50%     |
