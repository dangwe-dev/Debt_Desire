


#  The Upper Class

The upper class in Victoria refers to the citizens with the most means. They usually reside in the upper levels of the city this being from the 5th layer to the 7th layer.
The Upper class of Victoria owns all of the House Corporations and most of the Commercial Corporations in the city. This class has total control over the high Institution and the banks. Upper class members can always be found at the city's social club, "The Assembly Locals". 
To be considered an "Upper class" citizen, you must have a certain amount of income coming in every day. Going below that value will have you lose your standing. Holdings, stock, properties, all of these generate income. Having some is a form of guarantee. Debt, is irrelevant to the social class. You can have more debt that income and still be upper class. The challenge becomes maintaining appearances and avoiding debt collectors.

## Oligarchs

Oligarchs are upper class members with nobility titles. A nobility title in Victoria affects the entire family but they don't give their power to every member of that family. Only the title holder gets to benefit from the privileges. Nevertheless it does give perks and access to every family member without giving them the ability to brandish the power that comes with the title. Oligarchs are the members of Victoria who are the hardest to access and to befriend. They have very low tolerance and very high maintenance. On top of all of that they are snobs. If you don't have the proper attire and accessories, its going to be very hard to engage with an Oligarch.


## The Gentry

The Gentry, are upper class members without nobility titles. This sub class of upper society owns a portion of Commercial Corporations in Victoria, most of the Chartered Firms and contains some partnerships. Gentry families can be very rich and still accessible. Their goal is to get a nobility title following the social climbing environment of Victoria. With money and title in mind, they are more likely to engage with regular people who can benefit them. Though appearances still count for something they are more lenient when it comes to dealing with lower class people.





# The Middle Class

The middle class in Victoria represents the bulk of the population. Most of the citizens belonging to this class, are employees. The rest are either owners/business people or servants. Servants are the most trustworthy source of information in Victoria since they have the most proximity to the powerful. The business people in the middle class constitute the majority of Co-Operatives partners, they also are the actors in most of Victoria's partnerships and some chartered firms. These forms of corporate entity are all known for being held by the up and coming ambitious people in the city. The middle class is the most accessible class when it comes to socializing. Many of the citizens in this  class feel respectable and though it is the largest class group, it is also the group with the least amount of debt.


# The Underclass

The underclass represent the dark side of Victoria. The poorest in the city who often appear at night. Within them are criminal of many sorts. Robbers, thieves, murderers, assassins, You name it. Moving around at night is risky for this reason. The underclass are people who have fallen very low into debt and have yet to be arrested by debt collectors. Some of them still manage their debts by operating on the dark market. So its possible to make a come back from being in the underclass but its very hard. Its also possible to deal products with the underclass citizens though its very dangerous better be home at night if you don't want to risk dying.
