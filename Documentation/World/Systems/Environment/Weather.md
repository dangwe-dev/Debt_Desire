The weather conditions in Victoria, change with every day the city experiences. Weather, has a part to play in the game. It is the main deciding factor driving the production on lands. Every weather condition has a different effect on the lands in Victoria affecting their production capacity. So in addition to being an aesthetically pleasing visual effect, the weather in Victoria can serve as an indicator to which businesses are going to boom in the city's stock market.

# Atmospheric Conditions

There are 5 different types of atmospheric conditions randomly cycling each other through every day in the Debt & Desire world. Every one of these weather conditions, constantly benefit a specific type of land. 

**With the exceptions of pastures. Pastures being a type of land reserved for husbandry, its production is unaffected by the weather though it has its own variable causing random production.
