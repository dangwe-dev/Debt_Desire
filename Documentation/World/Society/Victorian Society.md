[[Victoria]]

Victorians, are a very proud people. Their city is home to mana dust. This precious material is the main ingredient needed in enchantments, magic tools and all sorts of magical tools. The main attraction of Victoria, is its large museum of magical history. The city being home to the creation of magical objects contributes tremendously to the pride the average Victorian emits. History and culture, all of these things are very costly in Victoria so only the richest in the city have easy access to knowledge and methods of production.
Despite the beauty of the city and its magical attractions, The people are very pompous and snob. What matters to Victorians are appearances and necessary material wealth to back up your appearance. This characteristic of the people makes the city full of ambitious superstitious people who will stop at nothing to gain more wealth. Even getting in atrocious amounts of debt, is better than losing face to a Victorian.


# Social Hierarchy


Victoria is a resource based industrial city. The whole economy of the city was made possible due to its location. The basin provides a  wide variety of resources. The powerful in the city own most of the natural resources the basin provides. This give them a tremendous amount of power and the ability to affect the flow of wealth in a very major way. The rich and powerful who lack nobility titles got to their stations by heading major sectors of industry or owning stock in various companies. Then there are merchants who also benefit from riches. They will be the owners of industries in a given sector. Finally the middle class will be composed of shop owners, craftsman and land workers. 
Employees and servants are at the bottom of the food chain.
