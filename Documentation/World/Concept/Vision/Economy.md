
The economy is the core of the game. Debt & Desire, is an idea about inventors in the Victorian era creating a new source of fuel powered by magic called the mana stone. This source of fuel disrupts the existing power dynamics and economy landscape. The players being the cause of this must survive debt and competition to ensure the city benefits from their invention and replace the syndicate as the economic power in Victoria.

For this experience to be felt the financial situations experienced by the players must be extremely volatile and punishing and at the same time rewarding and manageable. 

# Funds Behaviour

Having access to a stock market is not simply a quirky special addition to a societal simulation. The goal is to make the economic entities in the game swing between profit and loss. Every day has a different weather, this brings different outputs of natural resources, the companies relying on the estates are affected, the stock amrket moves. 
Of course weather is not the sole decider on if a company will take a dive. What it does is affect production. 
Reputation, will be another value affecting the market. It touches on how the owners of the businesses fare. How is their debt and income. The economy landscape is checked everyday in the game. So every day there are massive or slight changes but within those changes the players can go from riches to rags to riches again. This impacts the player's buying power and relationships constantly making the game chaotic and re-enforcing the manufactured feeling of being a Victorian era investor and inventor. 
**The times are for opportunity and gain, risk and loss are knocking at your door.

This last sentence feels like an accurate description of the desired financial conditions in this simulation.

So the buying power, the numbers seen on screen, all of these must contribute to this feeling of going from euphoria to despair due to the state of the wallet.
Considering debt means being wanted by the debt collectors, adding the feeling of being helpless even if you were not being chased completes the desired effect.

When the player is badly in debt and can't borrow from a bank, he becomes an underclass citizen and must borrow from loan sharks with even more absurd interest rates.
