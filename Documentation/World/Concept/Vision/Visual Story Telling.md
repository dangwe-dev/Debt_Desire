
The visuals in Debt & Desire are a big part of the gameplay. They don't simply contribute to making the game aesthetically pleasing. The goal is to make a multiplayer, co-op game. The accent is put on the gameplay and the dynamics with other players and NPC's. Little to no effort will be put into storytelling elements. It would require time and effort to create story elements. 
Nevertheless, it is important to create a narrative for immersion purposes. The players must feel like they are incarnating a live entity in another world. Having the feeling of entering a new world coupled with an attractive setting. These are elements that make a game attractive and contribute to returning players.
To achieve this effect, the game introduces many different visual mechanics and assets to suggest narratives to the players. Every facet of design is created with a lot of attention and intention. These intentional visual elements tell a story to the player and creates context without the need for dialogue, cut scenes or text windows. The player is an ambitious entrepreneur in a Victorian era city where social dynamics are hard to navigate through. This is the only thing the player needs to know in the beginning. Other contextual situations are instantly understood through visual language.

## Characters

The characters in Victoria, follow a certain set of aesthetic rules. These visual cues are put into place to allow players to make contextual decisions.
The social dynamics in Victoria are an important part of the gameplay. The player gets a feeling for the mood of the citizens by looking at their facial features. 
Every citizen follows a set of moods and these moods affect their facial expressions. The facial expressions the citizens make allow the player to make decisions on if and how to approach certain people.

The other set of visual cues allowing the player to navigate socially are clothes and haircuts. The wealthier a Victorian becomes, the more access he gains to luxury accessories. Looking at a citizen's outfit gives the player clues on social class. Every social class having its own behavioral dynamics, the player can base his decision making process on outfits to know which behavior to adopt.

## Environment

The visual design of Victoria aims to communicate social class and disparities at a glance. Every layer of the city as its own identity and as you go up you can appreciate complex architecture and larger buildings.

The city spawns from a procedural system. This means buildings have a defined consistent look all across town. 
There are at least 3 different procedural modules and from these modules the entire town is spawned.
Unique buildings are the seats of power, the academy etc. Any single structural entity needing specific architecture lives outside of the procedural rules.
