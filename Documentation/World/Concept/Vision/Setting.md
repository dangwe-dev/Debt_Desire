
The vision I had on September 22 2026 was of a world where plotting and backstabbing took place every day. I had this desire to create a setting where players would have to help each other navigate the complexities of court. 
The idea was enchanting to me. I love to explore power dynamics in social settings. The more I developed the idea the more I thought I would benefit from making the game a window into the world I have been writing for some years now. The initial idea quickly changed from a court setting to a metropolitan city in the Victorian era where citizens are hungry for material wealth and social standing. 
On October 1st 2026 I began to work on this project vault.
The code name for the project, is "Debt & Desire".

# Game Dynamic

"Debt & Desire", is a multiplayer co-op game where the players have for goal to become industry moguls and the most influential people in town.
The idea is to make a simulation where the players experience building a business from scratch.
The world of "Debt & Desire", introduces a city called "Victoria". This city is a metropolis where world defining innovations and advances in magical technology and fossil fuels take place.
The players, aim to make a name for themselves in the energy sector.
Like the name suggests, debt is a major part of the game.
The players can easily make a fortune in the game and just as easily lose it all.
The game to gives the players this feeling of constantly having to look at your wallet to make sure you are solvent. The players make or lose their fortunes through the city's stock market. The market being the unpredictable entity it is, gives the player a reason to constantly check on his money. The punishment for being complacent is loss of progress and funds.

The city is governed by the richest people. A group calling themselves "The Syndicate". This group controls the banks and the banks employ debt collectors. These entities have their own agency and once players or any other citizen falls into debt, the collectors target them. Being capture means a progress and fund penalty.

I want the players, to feel as if they are building a company in this fictional magical world. Hence relationships are important in gameplay. The two ways to earn money are through stock market investments and building your own company. To build your company, you must build positive relationships with the Victorian citizens to sell them your products. Your prestige decides if the Victorians want to do business with you. 
To improve your prestige you must spend money on yourself. Outfits and accessories for example can make you look more appealing to the power hungry and materialistic Victorians. So the loop becomes make investments, use the income to improve your self, leverage your improved self to make connections, sell your product to your connections and see your own stock rise.
