
The audio design in Debt & Desire, like in most games is indispensable to the user experience. 


## Characters

Characters emit sounds. They emit sounds when you cross their paths, when they walk, when they have discussions and if they are distressed. To minimize the amount of recordings needed for the voice of the characters, the sounds are reduced to exclamations, groans, whimpers, wails etc. The characters will emit sounds matching their emotional states. This strategy allows the players to hear the dynamics surrounding them. It also provides game cues.
