
Debt and Desire is the name for this project while it is in conception phase. The title of the game will probably be different but for not this is it.

Debt & Desire, is a simulation, multiplayer co-op game. The goal of the game is to rise in society by succeeding in the energy business of a Victorian era magical city.

The game is made within the Godot game engine and its visual components are created with blender.
